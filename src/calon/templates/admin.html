Calendars

Optional. calon checks conflicts against its own bookings either way. Connecting a calendar with OAuth adds its real free/busy and writes accepted bookings into it; subscribing to a published feed adds free/busy only, and needs no developer console.

Google OAuth redirect URI — register this exact URL as an authorized redirect URI on the OAuth client: {{ google_callback_url }}

Prefer the config file?
